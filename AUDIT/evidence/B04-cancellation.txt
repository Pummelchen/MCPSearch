Both mutations were reverted: SearchOrchestrator.swift has no `callerCancelledBefore`, and
DirectHTTPFetcher.swift throws CancellationError at both sites with WebFetcher's checkpoint
back in place.

## What is deliberately unchanged

Budget expiry is not cancellation. The orchestrator's own deadline still produces partial
results with warnings, because the deadline cancels provider *child* tasks, not the caller's;
the existing timeout and budget tests in SearchOrchestratorTests cover that and stay green.
