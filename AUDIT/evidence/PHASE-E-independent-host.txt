Two notes, recorded rather than smoothed over:

* **`osv-scanner` differs by a minor version** (2.6.0 on node2, 2.5.1 on node1 and in CI). Both
  report no issues for this dependency graph, so the gate's verdict is the same; the version is
  pinned in CI, which is where the reproducible number lives.
* **`brew install` exited 1 on node2** while installing `semgrep`'s dependencies: `certifi` and
  `pydantic` could not be symlinked because Homebrew's Python 3.14 site-packages already contain
  them. Every requested tool is installed and runs at the version above; the non-zero exit is the
  link step, not the install. No container was created, and nothing else was installed.

## What is *not* verified here

* **No vendor MCP client round trip.** Refused, not pending: providing an OpenAI or Anthropic
  account was declined, so the compatibility claim stays bounded to the documented rules, the
  schema lint and the protocol tests.
* **Live provider coverage is Tavily-only**, behind `SEARCH_LIVE_TESTS=1`. Every other adapter is
  verified against fixtures and the published API notes.
* **`CodeQL` is verified by CI, not here.** `B123` broke it by raising the manifest floor to 6.4
  (GitHub's default-setup runner builds with Swift 6.3.3 and cannot parse a 6.4 manifest); `B127`
  restored the 6.3 floor. The check can only be observed on the pull request, and its state at the
  time of writing is recorded in the ledger under `B127`.
