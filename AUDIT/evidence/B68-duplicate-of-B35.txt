`try await Task.sleep(for: .milliseconds(60))`. B35 (L6-13) reported and fixed it; B68 is the L3-18
report of the same two lines, kept separate when the findings were folded because the two passes
worded it differently.

```

(no matches)

        // (`ProviderHealth.recordFailure`), which `FusionAndReliabilityTests` asserts directly.

        // a reader who trusted that comment would reintroduce the race it described.
```

The ordering the old comment denied is pinned by
`FusionAndReliabilityTests.testOutcomesReachTheBreakerBeforeRecordReturns` (B35's evidence,
`AUDIT/evidence/B35-timing-couplings.txt`).

## Why this is a closure and not a narrowing

Nothing further is required to satisfy the finding: the dead wall-clock cost and the stale comment
are gone, and the property the comment misdescribed is asserted by a dedicated test. Recording the
duplicate here is the honest bookkeeping — the alternative, leaving it open, would claim work is
outstanding that is not.
